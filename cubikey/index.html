How to play

Easy to learn, hard to master: the first levels teach you, the later ones hide hundreds of arrows on two faces at once.

Turn

Swipe to rotate the shape and look for an arrow with a clear way out.

Tap

Tap it: it slides out over the edge. Blocked? You lose a heart.

Plan

Every arrow leaves in its own direction. Find the right order to free them all.

Boost

Stuck? The Eye shows a free arrow, Undo takes back a mistake, 3 Arrows clears three at once.
